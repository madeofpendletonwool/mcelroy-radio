About McElroy Radio

Welcome to McElroy Radio, a fan-made internet radio station dedicated to broadcasting the delightful chaos and warm humor of the McElroy family podcasts 24/7!

Whether you're a longtime fan of My Brother, My Brother and Me, The Adventure Zone, Sawbones, or any of the other wonderful McElroy shows, we've got you covered with a continuous stream of good good audio content.

How It Works

McElroy Radio operates like a traditional radio station - we play episodes continuously whether you're listening or not! Jump in anytime and catch whatever goof is currently being discussed. No need to choose what to listen to - we've got you covered.

Fan Project

This is a fan-made project created with love and appreciation for the McElroy family's content. We are not officially affiliated with the McElroy family or their podcasts.

Shows in Rotation

My Brother, My Brother and Me

An advice show for the modern era where three brothers help you solve your problems with their wisdom and unique perspective.

The Adventure Zone

Follow the McElroys as they play tabletop RPGs, creating unforgettable stories and characters along the way.

Sawbones

A marital tour of misguided medicine with Dr. Sydnee and Justin McElroy.

Wonderful!

Griffin and Rachel McElroy discuss the things that bring them joy and why they're so passionate about them.

Shmanners

Travis and Teresa McElroy explore the history and etiquette of various topics, offering practical advice with a touch of humor.

Still Buffering

Sisters Sydnee, Teylor, and Rileigh Smirl discuss the differences and similarities between their generations, covering topics from pop culture to personal experiences.

The Besties

Griffin and Justin McElroy, along with their friends Chris Plante and Russ Frushtick, review and discuss the latest in video games.

'Til Death Do Us Blart

An annual podcast where the McElroy brothers and hosts of The Worst Idea of All Time watch and review "Paul Blart: Mall Cop 2" every Thanksgiving.

Trends Like These

Travis McElroy and co-hosts discuss the latest trends and news stories from the internet, offering their insights and commentary.

Positiviteeny

Short, uplifting messages from Travis McElroy and friends, aiming to bring a little positivity to your day.

Run: A Doctor Who Fancast

Travis McElroy and Tybee Diskin delve into episodes of Doctor Who, sharing their thoughts and analyses.

Bunker Buddies

Travis McElroy and co-hosts discuss survival tactics and scenarios, blending humor with practical advice.

Interrobang

A podcast where the McElroy brothers explore various topics, answering listener questions with their signature humor.

CoolGames Inc.

Griffin and Nick Robinson brainstorm and pitch absurd video game ideas, blending comedy with creativity.

Quality Control

Justin McElroy discusses the latest video game reviews and industry news, offering his perspectives and critiques.

Surprisingly Nice

A podcast where Travis McElroy and co-hosts interview guests, aiming to highlight the good in people and their stories.

The Kind Rewind

The McElroy brothers revisit and discuss episodes of the TV show "Kindred: The Embraced," sharing their thoughts and memories.

The Empty Bowl

Rose Buddies
