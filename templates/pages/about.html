About McElroy Radio

Welcome to McElroy Radio, a fan-made internet radio station dedicated to broadcasting the delightful chaos and warm humor of the McElroy family podcasts 24/7!

Whether you're a longtime fan of My Brother, My Brother and Me, The Adventure Zone, Sawbones, or any of the other wonderful McElroy shows, we've got you covered with a continuous stream of good good audio content.

How It Works

McElroy Radio operates like a traditional radio station - we play episodes continuously whether you're listening or not! Jump in anytime and catch whatever goof is currently being discussed. No need to choose what to listen to - we've got you covered.

Fan Project

This is a fan-made project created with love and appreciation for the McElroy family's content. We are not officially affiliated with the McElroy family or their podcasts. For official McElroy content and updates, visit themcelroy.family .

About the Developer

madeofpendletonwool

McElroy Radio was lovingly crafted by a fellow McElroy fan who wanted to create the ultimate listening experience for the community. Built with Go, this project represents countless hours of development, fueled by good good content and an unhealthy amount of coffee.

For shows that no longer have official RSS feeds, I use ChopChopRSS , a custom-built RSS feed generator that creates podcast feeds from archived audio files. This allows me to bring discontinued shows back into rotation, ensuring that classic McElroy content remains accessible through modern podcast players and feeds. Want access to a functional feed for Joystiq or In Case of Emergency? Head over to The official McElroy Radio Feed to grab them. As other old shows are found they will be added here as well for perpetual archival.

I also develop and maintain Pinepods , a self-hosted podcast management platform that acts as a self-hosted Podcast archiver, listener, aggregator, and ultimate podcast platform.

This is a completely free, open-source project created for the love of the McElroy family's content and the amazing community around it. I do this for fun. Can't you just have fun for awhile?
